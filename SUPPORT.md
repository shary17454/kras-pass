# Kras Pass Support

Kras Pass is a local party mini-game collection for iPhone and iPad.

## Get Help

For questions, bug reports, or App Store support requests, contact the developer:

- Email: sharyalhwaid@gmail.com
- GitHub support page: https://github.com/shary17454/kras-pass

Please include:

- Your device model.
- iOS or iPadOS version.
- App version.
- A short description of what happened.

## Common Questions

### Does Kras Pass require an account?

No. Kras Pass does not require sign-in.

### Does Kras Pass collect personal data?

No. The app is designed as a local game and does not collect personal data.

### Does Kras Pass need camera, microphone, photo library, location, or contacts?

No. Current gameplay does not require those permissions.

### Can I play offline?

Yes. The main game modes are local and playable without an account.
