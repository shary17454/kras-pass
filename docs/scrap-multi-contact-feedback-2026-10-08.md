## Reproduced defect

Scrap's controller accepts each distinct live pair through its half-second
cooldown and deducts health. Fighter's ordinary hit immunity nevertheless
discarded the second accepted contact's impulse. With the same physical
positions, characters and velocities, exchanging two opponent seats changed
the surviving kart's combined impulse by 3.9671, without changing health.
The real-scene regression failed on the parent: 30 assertions passed, one
failed. This demonstrates contact-order dependence, not the cause of every
statistical seat warning.

The dedicated ram-feedback entry point reuses the existing hit implementation
but does not apply ordinary hit immunity a second time. Scrap still owns health
damage and per-pair cooldown. Ordinary hits retain immunity; dead bodies and
teammates remain rejected. No character, AI profile, threshold, seed or scoring
policy was changed. The existing five-argument virtual take_hit signature is
unchanged. Focused regression: 43 assertions passed after correcting the test's
Dictionary teammate setup.
