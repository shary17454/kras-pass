## Diagnosis and Scope

A development-only counterfactual runner neutralizes `knock_resist` at round
start and leaves other fighter fields unchanged. Its report and samples use
`counterfactual_resistance`, which the natural-evidence validator rejects.
The experiment changes resistance for all contacts, not only sweeper hits;
it is a causal diagnostic and is not the shipped behavior.

The shipped candidate instead compresses resistance sensitivity only when
Sweeper Storm's rotating arms deliver a hit. Other games' arms retain their
original strength (`resistance_influence=1`). Sweeper Storm uses 0.5, stored
in tuning data. Incoming strength is multiplied by the character/neutral
resistance ratio to the power of (1-influence), so the fighter's existing
resistance division leaves a square-root ratio. Heavy characters still resist
more than light characters, but the difference is reduced. Neutral strength,
fighter mass, character stats, perks, rival attacks, other hazards, jump
physics, AI, scoring, deadlines, seeds and acceptance thresholds are unchanged.
Mutator weight/resistance modifiers still pass through the fighter hit path.
