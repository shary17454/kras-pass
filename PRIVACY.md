# Kras Pass Privacy Policy

Kras Pass is a local party game. The app does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, achievements, and replay data locally on the device. This information stays on the device unless the user deletes the app or device backups include app data.

Kras Pass does not use advertising tracking, analytics tracking, third-party login, location services, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library access for gameplay.

For support, contact the developer through the repository support page:
https://github.com/shary17454/kras-pass/issues
